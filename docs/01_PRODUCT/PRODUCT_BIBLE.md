# Product Bible

## Vision

Turn SEO from fragmented manual work into a controlled personal production system that prioritizes opportunities with the highest probability of traffic, clicks, and conversion.

## Product principles

1. Do not create a page merely because a keyword exists.
2. Every asset must satisfy a real user intent.
3. Research and evidence precede generation.
4. Quality gates precede publication.
5. Performance data must influence future decisions.
6. Automation must reduce operator work without hiding decisions.

## Primary capabilities

- Market and competitor research
- Keyword, SERP, intent, and entity intelligence
- Opportunity scoring and prioritization
- Topic cluster and internal-link planning
- Research-backed content production
- Fact-checking and quality assurance
- CMS publishing
- Performance monitoring and content refresh

## Explicit non-goals for the personal edition

- Subscription billing
- White-label agency portals
- Enterprise SSO
- Multi-organization tenancy
- Complex team permissions
- Multi-region active-active infrastructure

## Success criteria

The system is successful when it helps one operator consistently identify worthwhile topics, publish better assets faster, reduce low-value output, and connect SEO activity to measurable outcomes.
