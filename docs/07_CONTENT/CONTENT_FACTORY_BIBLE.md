# Content Factory Bible

## Pipeline

Research → Evidence → Brief → Outline → Draft → Edit → Fact-check → SEO optimization → QA → Approval → Publish.

## Research

Research outputs must preserve source URLs, access dates, excerpts or notes, trust classification, and unresolved questions.

## Brief

Each brief defines:

- target intent and audience
- primary problem to solve
- required entities and subtopics
- differentiators from existing results
- evidence requirements
- conversion goal
- QA acceptance criteria

## Writing

The writer follows the brief and evidence package. It must avoid unsupported claims, generic filler, artificial repetition, and invented citations.

## Editing

Editing improves clarity, logical order, concision, usefulness, tone consistency, and alignment with the intended page type.

## Fact-checking

Claims are extracted and classified. Critical claims without sufficient evidence block publication or require explicit operator review.

## Versioning

Content versions are immutable snapshots with lineage, prompt/model provenance, diffs, QA results, and publication history.
