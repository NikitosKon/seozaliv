# SEO Engine Bible

## Objective

Identify and prioritize SEO work with the highest expected value rather than maximizing raw page count.

## Core modules

- Keyword discovery and normalization
- SERP snapshot collection
- Search intent classification
- Entity extraction and resolution
- Competitor intelligence
- Topic clustering
- Opportunity scoring
- Internal-link recommendations
- CTR and metadata optimization
- Freshness and content-decay detection

## Opportunity score

The score combines demand, competition, strategic relevance, conversion potential, confidence, and estimated execution cost. Every score must expose its component values and evidence.

## Page decision

An opportunity can produce one of four decisions:

- Create a new asset
- Update an existing asset
- Merge overlapping assets
- Reject or defer the topic

## Quality rules

- Preserve source dates and locale information.
- Distinguish measured provider data from model estimates.
- Detect mixed intent and possible cannibalization.
- Avoid treating search volume as the only indication of value.

## Feedback loop

Search Console, ranking, traffic, CTR, conversion, and content freshness data feed back into opportunity scoring and refresh recommendations.
